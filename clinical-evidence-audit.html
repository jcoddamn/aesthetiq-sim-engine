Important limitations

Clinical photos from publications and galleries are not automatically licensed for commercial reuse. Do not upload copyrighted or non-consensual patient images. Results require matching camera angle, expression, lighting and time after treatment. Skin, hair, dental and body procedures need different measurement models and will be reported as not yet quantitatively comparable.
