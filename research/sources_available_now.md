## Lip styles

All seven presets remain clinically unvalidated. Classic Volume has general augmentation references; Heart Lips has a related modified Cupid’s-bow source. Technique papers do not directly validate Russian Lips. Hydration, Pillow, Keyhole and Glossy Contour still lack matched evidence.

## What this batch actually completed

- Checked article-level CC BY licenses and adult eligibility statements in primary sources.
- Acquired and visually inspected three published under-eye surgical pairs. All lack the full-face reference regions required by the existing measurement pipeline; none was used to fit simulation strengths.
- Added two published aggregate research benchmarks: Cupid’s-bow morphology and under-eye filler clinical scores. These are not patient-level simulation comparisons.
- Image access failed for the new lip sources in this session; those remain retrieval/quality-review candidates.
- No new simulator comparisons, no clinical calibration, and no photos in the app.

## Source handling

Keep study, figure, author attribution, procedure, technique, product/dose and follow-up separate. Cohort enrollment does not equal downloadable case count. Repeated follow-ups are not independent people. Article licensing does not establish suitability for every purpose. No training, redistribution or permanent facial dataset is authorized by this register.

The two chin studies reviewed (PMC9241653 and PMC13075509) have noncommercial restrictions and were excluded. The anatomy review’s patient photographs were not acquired because the pictured adults’ ages were not established.
