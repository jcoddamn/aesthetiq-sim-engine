Before you begin

A preview is a conversation starter.

AesthetIQ helps adults explore cosmetic procedures and prepare questions for a qualified clinician. It does not diagnose conditions, determine eligibility or predict a surgical result.

Choose a starting point

Explore the library for procedure goals, recovery and risks. Save the procedures you want to revisit. From a procedure’s detail page, you can open its available preview and options.

Understand your preview

Natural, Balanced and Enhanced are visual intensity settings. They are not treatment doses or guaranteed outcomes. Recovery displays are illustrations, not a personal healing forecast. Body previews approximate a 2D contour; the 3D viewer is an illustrative model.

Measurement definitions help describe changes, but the app’s simulated outcomes have not been clinically validated. A photograph alone cannot establish tissue volume or treatment suitability.

Get a clearer image

Use even lighting, a neutral expression and an unobstructed face. Keep the camera at eye level. If the camera is unavailable, choose a photo instead. You can change camera permissions in your browser or device settings.

Your data in this version

Saved procedure IDs are kept in this browser’s local storage and do not sync to an account. Camera and photo processing runs in the browser. The preview pages load third-party model and rendering assets, so they still make network requests.

If you approve transferring a Precision Scan to the 3D viewer, the app stores its photos and landmarks locally in this browser. The scan becomes ineligible for loading after 30 minutes and is deleted when the app next checks it; this is not a background deletion timer. Use “Delete local scan” in the 3D viewer to remove it sooner.

Downloaded images remain on your device until you delete them. Clearing this site’s browser data removes locally saved lists and scan data. Only use your own images or images you have permission to process.

Take the next step thoughtfully

Bring your goals and questions to a qualified clinician. Ask about suitability, alternatives, recovery, risks and realistic results for you.
