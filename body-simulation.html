Body Preview

Upload a well-lit front-facing or rear-facing body photo. AesthetIQ uses MediaPipe Pose and person segmentation to create an illustrative contour preview. This is not an accurate surgical prediction, medical assessment, or implant sizing tool.

Select a photo to begin.

Limitations

These simulations use a 2D silhouette approximation. MediaPipe Pose estimates skeletal landmarks; person segmentation does not identify breast tissue, fat, muscle, skin laxity, surgical planes, or implant shape. Clothing, occlusion, camera angle, and lighting can affect results. No uploaded photo is sent to AesthetIQ by this page, but third-party MediaPipe assets load from a CDN.
